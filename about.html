About Matjar

Who We Are

Matjar is a Saudi-based e-commerce platform that allows customers to browse, search, and purchase products online. We are dedicated to providing the best shopping experience for our customers across Saudi Arabia.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make online shopping easy, safe, and affordable for everyone in Saudi Arabia. We aim to connect customers with high-quality products at competitive prices, with fast and reliable delivery.

What We Offer

  • Fashion: Men's, women's, and children's clothing, shoes, and bags
  • Home & Kitchen: Furniture, appliances, and home decor
  • Grocery: Snacks, beverages, and personal care products
